About us

Welcome to Thien Thi Tailor

Thien Thi Tailor is run by Thi and her sisters, in a yellow house with arched windows on Phan Bội Châu. We make dresses, tops, suits and linen to your exact measurements, pick accessories to match your outfit and take care of repairs and alterations.

Bring the design you have in mind, even a complicated one, or a garment you'd like copied. Browse our big catalogue of designs and the walls of fabric, and we'll advise you patiently. A dress is often ready in about two days, and sometimes a top by the same evening.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring?

A photo, a screenshot or a garment similar to what you want. No idea yet? Browse our catalogue of designs and we'll suggest what suits you.

How long does it take?

A dress usually takes about two days, and simpler pieces can be quicker: one guest ordered a top in the morning and collected it at 7 pm. Tell us when you leave and we'll plan the fittings.

Can you copy my favourite clothes?

Yes. Bring the piece, choose a fabric, and we'll make a copy in your size. We also repair and alter clothes.

Do I need to book?

You're welcome to drop in, but if you book a time we can give you our full attention. Pick a time online and we'll confirm on WhatsApp or Zalo.
