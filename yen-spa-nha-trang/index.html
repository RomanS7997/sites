About us

Welcome to Yên Spa Nha Trang

Yên Spa is a cozy massage studio in alley 132 on Hùng Vương street, in the centre of Nha Trang. Before the massage you fill in a short form about your wishes, problem areas and the pressure you like, and we bring you cold tea and a warm foot bath.

Guests write about hot stones, aroma oil, warm compresses and caring, very polite therapists. After the massage there is tea and yogurt. Many come back on the next trip.
