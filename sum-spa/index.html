About us

Welcome to Sum Spa

Sum Spa is on Lam Hoanh Street in An Hai, 50 metres from My Khe Beach. Our motto is “Open your mind, relax your body”. You're greeted with tea and a cool towel, your visit starts with a herbal foot bath, and the massage happens in a private room. Afterwards there's hot herbal tea and seasonal fruit, a shower if you need one and a safe spot for your luggage.

Guests from all over the world have left 4,897 reviews on Google with an average of 4.9. They write about therapists who keep asking if the pressure is right, a spotless spa and the warm ginger tea at the end.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the massage oil?

Yes. For aroma treatments you pick a scent before you start: peony, orange, coffee, lemongrass or peppermint.

Can we come as a couple or a family?

Of course. Couples, friends and families come together all the time. Choose the number of people in the booking form, or message us and we'll set it up.

Can I come right before my flight?

Yes. You can take a shower after your treatment and leave your luggage with us.

How do I book?

Pick a time in the form on this page, or message us on WhatsApp, Zalo, KakaoTalk, LINE or WeChat. We're at 15 Lam Hoanh, 50 m from My Khe Beach.
