About us

Welcome to Ribbonz Nail & Spa

Ribbonz is a pink-and-lilac nail & spa salon in Sunset Town, Phu Quoc, run by a friendly Korean owner. Come for a manicure or pedicure with gel polish and cute designs, a foot massage after a day of walking, or a full aroma massage.

Guests write about the friendly owner, the welcome tea and how clean and cozy the salon is. After a body massage you can use our private shower, and we can pick you up and drop you off at your hotel.
