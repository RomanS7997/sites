About us

Welcome to Happy Tam Coc Ecolodge

Happy Tam Coc Ecolodge is a family-run lodge at the end of a quiet road outside Tam Coc, right under the limestone cliffs. Rooms, suites and a villa with its own pool stand in a large garden with a pool, a lake, a red bridge and a pavilion.

Lily and her family look after every guest: bus and boat tickets, scooters, a driver for the day. Breakfast is served in the gazebo, our restaurant cooks Vietnamese and British dishes, and bicycles are free. The Tam Coc boat wharf is 9 minutes by car, and Bich Dong Pagoda and Thung Nang are close by.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 23:30 and check-out is until 12:00. Please tell us your arrival time in advance.

How far is the Tam Coc boat trip?

The Tam Coc boat wharf is 9 minutes by car, or a short ride on our free bicycles. Bich Dong Pagoda and Thung Nang are close by.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of any age are welcome, and there is a playground. We have no cribs; an extra bed for a child aged 6–11 costs 260,000 VND per night on request.

Can a big family or group stay together?

Yes: book the whole villa with a private pool for up to 16 guests. Message us on WhatsApp with your dates.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, pets are welcome and stay free.

How can I pay?

In cash at the lodge. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price for your stay.
