About us

Welcome to THANH Design

THANH Design is a custom tailor at 75 Trần Hưng Đạo, in the heart of Hoi An. Show us a photo of a jacket, shirt or dress you love, or bring a piece you already own. We'll tell you honestly what works, help you choose the fabric and take your measurements.

At the fitting you move, sit and turn, so the clothes feel good in real life, not only when standing still. We adjust until everything sits right, and guests often write about suits made in a few hours or ready the next day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

It depends on the piece and the season. Guests have had suits made in a few hours, or picked them up the next day after a final fitting. Tell us your departure date when you book and we'll plan the fittings around it.

Can you copy a photo or my own clothes?

Yes. Bring Pinterest or Instagram pictures, or a garment you love: one guest had the shorts he was wearing copied exactly. We'll tell you honestly what works with the fabric you pick.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the fabric and the design. Send us a photo on WhatsApp or Zalo and we'll suggest fabrics and give you a quote before you come.

Do I need to book?

You can simply walk in, but booking a measuring time saves waiting, especially when your trip is short. Pick a time online and we'll confirm on WhatsApp or Zalo.
