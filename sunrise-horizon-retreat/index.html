About us

Welcome to Sunrise Horizon Retreat

Sunrise Horizon Retreat stands right on the bank of the Son River in Phong Nha. From the rooms and the terrace you see the river, the boats leaving for Phong Nha Cave and the limestone mountains, and every evening the sunset over the water.

Levi and his family run the retreat themselves. Our kitchen cooks Vietnamese dishes, with vegetarian and vegan options, and we help with everything else: scooters, SUP boards, cave tours and bus tickets, with pick-up right at the door.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 until midnight, check-out from 10:00 to 12:00. Arriving on an early bus? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is breakfast included?

Usually yes. Ask us on WhatsApp for your dates. Our restaurant is open for lunch and dinner too.

How do I get to the caves?

The boats to Phong Nha Cave leave from the river right next to us. For Paradise Cave and Dark Cave we help with a scooter or a tour.

Are children and pets welcome?

Yes. Children of all ages are welcome, cots depend on the room, and there are no extra beds. Pets stay free.

How can I pay?

Cash or card. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price for your stay.
