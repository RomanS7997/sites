About us

Welcome to Cat Ba Swiss Hotel

Cat Ba Swiss Hotel is a family-run hotel on Nui Ngoc hill, a short walk from the centre of Cat Ba town and still quiet at night. The rooms are modern and bright, 23 to 28 m², on high floors with a view over the town.

Billy and his family run the hotel themselves. In the morning there is pho, fresh fruit, bread and coffee; in the evening the lounge with blue velvet sofas and a piano. Ask us what to see on the island: guests write that Billy's tips and family dinners became the best memory of their trip.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 23:30, check-out until 12:00. Book direct on this website and you can check in from 10:00 and check out until 14:00 for free, depending on free rooms.

How big is the discount for booking direct?

15% off when you book direct on this website, and an extra 10% off if you stay 7 nights or more.

Is breakfast available?

Yes: pho, fresh fruit, bread, eggs, coffee and tea. Ask on WhatsApp whether it is included for your dates.

How far are the town centre and the beaches?

The town centre is a short walk down the hill, the Cat Co beaches about 15 minutes on foot. Ask us for tips on Lan Ha Bay and Cat Ba National Park.

Are children welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome; from 7 years old they count as adults. There are no cribs or extra beds, so families choose the room with two queen beds.

How can I book and pay?

Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price and confirm your room right in the chat.
