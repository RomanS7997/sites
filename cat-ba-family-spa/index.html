About us

Welcome to Cat Ba Family Spa

Cat Ba Family Spa is a family-run spa and hair salon on 1/4 street in Cat Ba town. Victoria, Lisa and the family do massage, hot stones, cupping, facials and the long Vietnamese hair wash that guests love most.

Travellers come after the Ha Giang loop or a few days on a motorbike, and write that they were treated like family: tea, fruit and snacks, a warm foot bath, a clean quiet room and the right pressure for each guest.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do guests choose most?

The 120-minute combo: full body massage, facial care and hair wash, 600,000 VND. For a shorter visit, take the 60-minute facial and hair wash.

Do I need to book?

You can walk in when a therapist is free. For a set time or a group, book online here in your language or message us on WhatsApp or Zalo.

Can I choose soft or strong pressure?

Yes. Tell the therapist what you like at the start, and say if you want it softer or stronger during the massage.
