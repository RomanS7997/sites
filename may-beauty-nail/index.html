About us

Welcome to Mây Beauty Nail

Mây Beauty is a small, bright nail and beauty salon on Nguyễn Trung Trực street, near Dinh Cậu, run by Quyên. Besides nails we do eyelash extensions, Vietnamese herbal head spa, foot and body care and skincare.

Guests write about kind, gentle technicians who are patient while you choose from many colours, help with suggestions and use a translator if you do not speak Vietnamese. Prices are reasonable, and you get water during your treatment.
