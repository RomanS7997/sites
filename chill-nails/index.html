About us

Welcome to Chill Nails

Chill Nails is a small nail room at 16 Nguyễn Thái Bình, Phu Quoc. Show us a photo on your phone or pick a design from our Instagram, and our technicians recreate it by hand, changing any details you like.

We do gel polish, Gel X, builder gel (BIAB) and no-tip dual form extensions, plus designs from ombre and chrome to 3D art and charms. Every price is on this page, and every set comes with a 7-day repair warranty.
