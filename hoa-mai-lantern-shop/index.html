About us

Welcome to Hoa Mai Lantern Shop

Hoa Mai is a lantern shop at 10/26 Bạch Đằng, down a little lane a short walk from the Hoi An market. Silk lanterns in every colour hang from the walls and the ceiling, and right here in the shop you can sit down and make your own.

Lanh shows you each step: you choose the silk, glue it onto a bamboo frame and finish the details. Guests say it takes 30–60 minutes, and even beginners manage it easily. In a hurry? Just drop in and pick a ready-made lantern to take home.
