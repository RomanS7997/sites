About us

Welcome to Casa Nail Spa

Casa Nail Spa is a calm, bright salon at 42 Mai Thúc Lân, near My Khe beach. Pedicure chairs with daisy cushions, a whole wall of gel colours and a team that takes its time: guests write that everything is explained clearly and every question gets a patient answer.

Bring a photo of a design you like, or choose one of ours. Besides nails we do lash extensions, lash lift, brow lamination and foot care with scrub, mask and massage. Every price is on this page, so you know the cost before you sit down.
