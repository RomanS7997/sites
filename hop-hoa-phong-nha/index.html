About us

Welcome to Hợp Hoa Massage

Hợp Hoa is a family massage spa in Phong Nha, run at home by three sisters. Hoa and her sisters do body and foot massage, hot stones, neck and shoulders, face massage and a nourishing hair wash.

Guests come after the caves and the hills of Phong Nha. Before the massage you get hot tea and a warm herbal foot bath, and after it passion fruit juice and fresh fruit. On a cold day, ask for a warm room.

Frequently asked questions

When are you open?

We are open 24 hours. Please message us about 30 minutes before you come, so we can get the room and the foot bath ready. Or book online here in your language.

Can you pick me up from my hotel?

Message us on WhatsApp or Zalo with your hotel name, and we will tell you if we can pick you up.

Where are you?

In Phong Nha town. From the road, follow our purple sign “Massage Phong Nha Hợp Hoa · 150 m”. The map is below.

It is cold. Do you have a warm room?

Yes, just ask for a warm room when you book. In winter Phong Nha can be cold and damp after the caves.
