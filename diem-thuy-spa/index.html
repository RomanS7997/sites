About us

Welcome to Điềm Thụy Spa

Điềm Thụy Spa was born from a love of Da Lat's quiet beauty. In Vietnamese, «Điềm» means stillness and peace, and «Thụy» means deep, restful sleep. That is the feeling we want you to leave with.

We combine Eastern herbal healing with modern therapy: hot stones, sandalwood tools, mugwort and cupping, herbal baths and hair washes. Every room is decorated with paintings, so a visit feels like resting in a small gallery.
