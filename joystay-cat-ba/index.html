About us

Welcome to JoyStay Cat Ba

JoyStay is a family bungalow stay in Xuan Dam village on Cat Ba Island, run by two brothers, Minh and his brother. We have two gardens 100 m apart: JoyStay with 7 thatched A-frame bungalows, and the newer Ra Village with 10 wooden bungalows looking out at the garden.

Our restaurant cooks breakfast, lunch and dinner, with vegetarian and vegan dishes, and there is a bar and a coffee corner. Xuan Dam beach is a 5-minute walk away, and the bus to Cat Ba town stops nearby. We help book boat trips around the bay and bus tickets to Hanoi.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 12:00. Please tell us your arrival time in advance on WhatsApp.

What is the difference between JoyStay and Ra Village?

They are 100 m apart and share the same hosts and restaurant. JoyStay has 7 thatched A-frame bungalows with a loft; Ra Village has 10 newer, more spacious wooden bungalows with garden views and a hot tub.

How do I get to Cat Ba town?

The public bus to Cat Ba town stops nearby, or we can help you rent a scooter or call a taxi. Just ask us.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children over 1 year are welcome. We have no cribs or extra beds, so for a family choose a Family Bungalow.

Can I bring my pet?

Sorry, pets are not allowed.

How can I pay?

We accept cash. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price for your stay.
