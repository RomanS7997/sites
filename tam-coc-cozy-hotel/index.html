About us

Welcome to Tam Coc Cozy Hotel

Tam Coc Cozy Hotel is a new family-run hotel on the main road of the Tam Coc tourist area. Everything here is brand new: large bright rooms with marble floors, beds made of lim ironwood, air conditioning, a TV, a fridge and modern bathrooms.

We run the hotel ourselves and look after every guest. Breakfast is served in our dining room, an elevator takes you to every floor, and parking is free. Restaurants, bars and shops are a few minutes' walk away.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 23:30 and check-out is until 12:00. Arriving at another time? Message us on WhatsApp.

How big are the rooms?

The room for two is 25 m² with one large bed. Rooms for four have two large beds; the garden-view room is 32 m².

Is breakfast available?

Yes, we serve breakfast every morning in our dining room. Ask us on WhatsApp what is included for your dates.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. From 13 years they count as adults. We have no cribs or extra beds, so a family of four should choose a room for four.

Can I bring my pet?

Sorry, pets are not allowed.

How can I pay?

Cash or card. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price for your stay.
