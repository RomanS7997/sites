About us

Welcome to Cắt Tóc Seoul

A Korean-style barbershop and spa on Tran Hung Dao Street, a few minutes' walk from Hoi An's Old Town. Our signature is the full course: a herbal foot bath, shave, stone face massage, ear cleaning, scalp and full-body massage, Thai-style stretching, then a wash and blow-dry.

Guests love the ear cleaning and how clean the place is, and many come with their parents or kids. After your treatment we bring you a mango juice. 4,326 guests have rated us 4.9 on Google.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay in Korean won?

Yes. You can pay in won and get your change in dong. Dong is fine too, of course.

Can women do the full course?

Of course. We skip the shave for women and add more massage instead. If you'd rather we didn't touch your face, just tell us and we'll spend that time on another area.

Can I leave my suitcase with you?

Yes, we'll look after your luggage while you explore the Old Town. Guests like to drop by at the start or the end of a trip.
