About us

Welcome to Nón Spa Hội An

Nón Spa is a massage and beauty spa in an old yellow house at 17 Le Loi, in the Ancient Town of Hoi An. Nón is the Vietnamese conical hat, and you'll find a row of them glowing above our reception desk. We offer Thai, Vietnamese, aroma and hot stone massage, foot massage, organic facials, body scrubs, waxing and nails.

Guests from all over the world have left 3,282 reviews on Google, with an average of 4.9. They love the welcome: slippers, a cold towel, cold water and a lime foot soak as soon as you sit down, then lemon iced tea and coconut yogurt after your massage. And they come back for our friendly, skilled therapists.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you pick up from my hotel?

Yes, pick-up and drop-off are free anywhere in the Hoi An area. Send us your hotel name and the time on WhatsApp, Zalo, KakaoTalk or LINE.

Which massage should I choose?

For firm pressure and stretching, try the Thai massage. For something softer, the Vietnamese or aroma massage. Can't decide? The Nón Spa Signature combines Thai, Vietnamese and hot stones, and we'll gladly help you choose.

Can we come as a couple or with friends?

Of course. We have air-conditioned private rooms, so you can enjoy your massage together. Choose the number of guests when you book, or message us for a bigger group.

Where are you and when are you open?

17 Le Loi, in the Ancient Town of Hoi An. We're open every day from 8:30 to 22:30. The entrance and restroom are wheelchair accessible.
