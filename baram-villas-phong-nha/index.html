About us

Welcome to Baram Villas Phong Nha

Baram Villas is a new place in Ha Loi village near Phong Nha. Bright modern rooms with balconies look over the pool, the garden and the limestone mountains; the family rooms are 45 m², one with a bathtub right by the window.

We look after every guest ourselves: breakfast cooked to your needs, free bicycles, SUP boards and kayaks for the river, a pool table in the lounge, and help with cave tours and buses. Leaving on a night bus? We help with that too.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

You can check in until 23:00, our front desk works 24 hours. Check-out is until 12:00.

Is breakfast available?

Yes, an Asian breakfast every morning. Tell us about allergies or diets and we will adapt it. Ask on WhatsApp what is included for your dates.

How far are the caves?

Phong Nha town and the boats to Phong Nha Cave are a short ride away. We help with tours to Paradise Cave and Dark Cave and with buses.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome; we have no cribs or extra beds, so families choose a 45 m² family room. Sorry, pets are not allowed.

How can I book and pay?

Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price and confirm your room right in the chat.
