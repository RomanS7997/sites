About us

Welcome to Sen Việt Spa

Sen Việt Spa is a spacious spa on several floors at 287 Nguyễn Thái Học in Quy Nhon. Come for a body or foot massage, an herbal hair wash the traditional Vietnamese way, or facial and skin care in a private room.

Guests write that the whole team is well trained, the towels and massage tables are clean, and the therapists adjust the massage to what you ask for. Book online in your language: we are open every day from 8:00 to 21:00.
