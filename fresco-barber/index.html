About us

Welcome to Fresco Barber

Fresco Barber is a clean, new barbershop at 50 Thuốc Bắc, in the heart of Hanoi's Old Quarter. Show us a photo of the cut you want, and our young team will get it right. Guests also come for the beard trim, the clean shave and the nose wax.

Quân and the team speak English, so it's easy to explain what you want and get honest advice for your hair. Prices are clear before we start, so you know exactly what you pay.
