About us

Welcome to Jenny's Spa & Nail

Jenny's opened in Nha Trang in 2020 as Jenny's Nails Room and has since grown into Jenny's Spa & Nail: a calm space with plaster walls, rattan lanterns and a green velvet sofa, where massage and nails are under one roof.

Guests rate us 4.9 on Google across 2,407 reviews. They mention neat nail designs that match the palette exactly, Vietnamese and Thai massages that melt away travel fatigue, and a kind team who happily answer questions about Vietnam.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I do massage and nails in one visit?

Yes, that's what we're known for. Mention both services when you book and we'll plan the time so you don't have to wait.

Which massage should I choose?

For tired legs and back after a day out, try the Vietnamese traditional massage. Prefer stretching? Choose Thai. For deep warmth, go for the aroma hot stone massage.

Can I bring my child?

Of course. Kids can have a gentle massage, and the whole family is welcome. Just tell us how many people when you book.

Do I need to book, and where are you?

Booking is the best way to get the time you want. Choose a time online or message us on KakaoTalk, WhatsApp or Zalo. We're at 67 Bach Dang, central Nha Trang, open daily 9:00–22:00.
