About us

Welcome to Napoli Hostel Cat Ba

Napoli Hostel is a family-run hostel in the middle of Cat Ba town, on Nui Ngoc street. Rooms are new and bright, most with a balcony, some looking out at the green hills. Downstairs there is a lobby with a pool table, a bar and our restaurant.

Gem and her family run the hostel and help with everything on the island: boat trips around Lan Ha Bay with kayaking and swimming, scooter rental, bus tickets to Hanoi and your next stop. There is an ATM right here, and you can pay by card or in cash.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Please tell us your arrival time in advance on WhatsApp.

How far are the beaches?

We are in Cat Ba town. Cat Co 2 beach is about 950 m away, Cat Co 1 about 1.3 km, and Cannon Fort 750 m.

Can you book boat trips and bus tickets?

Yes. At our desk you can book Lan Ha Bay and Ha Long Bay trips with pick-up from the hostel, rent a scooter and buy bus tickets to Hanoi and other cities.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. We have no cribs or extra beds, so for a family of four choose a room for four.

Can I bring my pet?

Sorry, pets are not allowed.

How can I pay?

Cash or card, and there is an ATM right at the hostel. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price for your stay.
