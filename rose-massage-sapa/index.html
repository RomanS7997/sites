About us

Welcome to Rose Massage

Rose Massage is a cosy massage spa on Lương Định Của street in Sa Pa, a few minutes from the lake, open 24 hours. Rosie and her team do foot and body massage, hot stones, cupping and the traditional Red Dao herbal bath in a wooden tub.

Guests come after trekking to Fansipan or Cat Cat village, or after a day on a motorbike. They write about kind, attentive therapists, hot tea and sweets after the massage, fair prices and discounts for groups.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you open at night?

Yes, we are open 24 hours, every day. You can walk in, or book online here in your language or message us on WhatsApp or Zalo.

What is the Red Dao herbal bath?

A traditional bath of the Red Dao people of Sa Pa: you soak in a wooden tub of hot water with mountain herbs. A hair wash is included. You can add a body massage as a combo.

Where are you?

At 54 Lương Định Của, a few minutes' walk from Sa Pa lake. Look for the brown “Rose Massage & Spa” sign. The map is below.

Do you have discounts for groups?

Groups and families often get a discount. Message us on WhatsApp or Zalo before you come and we will tell you.
