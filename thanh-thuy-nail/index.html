About us

Welcome to Thanh Thuy Nail Beauty & Academy

Thanh Thuy Nail & Academy is a nail salon and a nail school on Võ Văn Kiệt street in An Hải, Da Nang. We do gel polish, BIAB, extensions and hand-painted nail art, from soft pastel sets to 3D cats, flowers and chrome.

Guests write about the kind owner who picks colours that suit your skin, quick neat work and fair prices. Bring a photo or an idea and we will turn it into your set. Book online in your language.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I find you?

We are in alley 74 Võ Văn Kiệt, An Hải. At the alley entrance there is a small sign “Thanh Thuy Nails · 10m”, the salon is a few steps in. The map is below.

Do I need to book?

You can walk in when a table is free. To be sure of your time, book online here in your language or message us on Zalo.

How much does a manicure cost?

Solid colour gel is 99,000 VND, cat eye or BIAB 120,000 VND, extensions from 80,000 VND. Nail art is added on top, from 5,000 VND. All prices are in the menu above.

Do you teach nail courses?

Yes, Thanh Thuy is also a nail academy. Message us on Zalo for the course, schedule and fee.
