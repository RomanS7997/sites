About us

Welcome to Se Se Tailor

Se Se is a family tailor shop and cloth store on Nguyễn Duy Hiệu street in Hoi An, run by sisters Sương, Anna and Nhi. Guests come with a photo or an idea, choose the fabric and the lining, and pick up a finished suit or dress a day or two later, after a fitting and any changes they want.

In over 1,300 reviews guests write about kind, patient staff who speak English, help you choose colours that suit you and never rush you. Some guests come on the advice of their hotel or a travel blog.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Usually one to two days: measurements on the first day, a fitting the next day, then the finished piece. Tell us your departure date and we plan around it.

How much does a suit or dress cost?

It depends on the fabric and the design. Send us a photo on WhatsApp or Zalo and we will tell you the price before you come in.

Can you copy a piece from a photo?

Yes, most guests bring a photo or a screenshot. You can also pick a style from the mannequins in the window and change the details.

Where are you?

At 311 Nguyễn Duy Hiệu, a short walk from the Old Town. Look for the gold SE SE sign. The map is below.
