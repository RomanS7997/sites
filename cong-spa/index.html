About us

Welcome to Cong Spa

A massage and nail spa on An Hoi islet, a short walk over the bridge from Hoi An's Old Town. Before your massage you fill in a short form: how strong you like it, which areas need a gentle touch and where to focus. Your therapist follows it and checks in with you along the way.

After the treatment, fresh watermelon is waiting for you. Many guests come back night after night during their stay, and 2,349 of them have given us 5.0 on Google.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose how strong the massage is?

Yes. Before you start, you fill in a short form with the pressure you like and the areas to focus on or avoid. You can also tell your therapist at any time.

How late are you open?

Every day from 9:00 to 23:30, and until midnight on Tuesdays and Sundays.

Can we come as a group or a family?

Of course. Our beds stand side by side, so friends and families can have their massage together. Book online for up to 4 people, or message us for a bigger group.

How do I book?

Pick a time in the form below and we'll confirm on WhatsApp or Zalo. You can also add us on KakaoTalk: kevin22c.
