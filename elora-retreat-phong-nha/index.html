About us

Welcome to Elora Retreat Phong Nha

Elora Retreat is a family-run retreat at the foot of the limestone mountains of Phong Nha. Yellow bungalows with arched doors stand around a swimming pool among palms, and the rooms upstairs open onto balconies facing the mountains.

Every room is large, 35 to 40 m², with bathrobes and a walk-in shower, some with a bathtub. Our kitchen cooks to order, bicycles are free, and the family helps with caves, tours and late departures.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 23:00. Our front desk works 24 hours, so tell us on WhatsApp when you plan to leave.

What is the difference between a bungalow and a room?

Bungalows are separate houses on the ground floor around the pool, with their own patio. Rooms are upstairs, with a balcony and a view of the mountains or the garden.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. An extra bed for guests from 12 years is available on request for 350,000 ₫ a night.

How far are the caves?

The boats to Phong Nha Cave leave from the town a short ride away. For Paradise Cave and Dark Cave we help with transport or a tour.

How can I book and pay?

Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price and confirm your room right in the chat.
